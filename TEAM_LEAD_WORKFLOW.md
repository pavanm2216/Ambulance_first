# Ambulance First — Team Lead Workflow

Implemented Team Lead operational surface based on the supplied workflow analysis.

## Navigation
- Operations Dashboard
- Pending Allocation
- Budget & Quotations
- Live Trips & Tracking
- Ambulance Fleet
- Driver Roster
- EMT / Paramedic Roster
- Doctor Roster
- Operations Reports

## Workflow
Customer Care handoff → resource allocation → budget/quotation → customer response → dispatch/trip monitoring → completion.

Allocation supports ambulance capability checks conceptually for oxygen, ICU, ventilator, pediatric ICU, cardiac monitor, stretcher, wheelchair and freezer requirements. Dead-body transport is represented with freezer capability.
